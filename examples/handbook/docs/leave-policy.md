# Leave policy

## Holidays

- Full-time staff get 26 days of paid holiday a year, plus public holidays.
- Part-time staff get holiday in proportion to their contracted hours.
- Up to 5 unused days carry over to the next year; they expire on 31 March.
- Request holidays in the HR system at least two weeks ahead for anything
  longer than three days. Your manager answers within three working days.

## Studio closure

The studio closes between Christmas and New Year. These days do not count
against your holiday allowance.

## Sick leave

- Tell your manager before 10:00 on the first day you are sick.
- Up to three days in a row need no doctor's note; longer absences do.
- Sick days are paid in full for up to 30 days a year.

## Parental leave

- Birth parents get 16 weeks of fully paid leave.
- Partners and adoptive parents get 8 weeks of fully paid leave.
- Leave can be split into up to three blocks within the child's first year.

## Other leave

- 2 days of paid leave for moving house, once a year.
- Up to 5 days of paid compassionate leave; talk to People Ops if you need more.
