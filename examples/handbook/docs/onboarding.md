# Onboarding

Welcome to Brightwater. This page walks you through your first two weeks.

## Before day one

- People Ops sends your laptop and a welcome pack to your home address.
- You get an email invite to Google Workspace, Slack and Figma.
- Your manager picks an onboarding buddy from another team.

## Week one

1. Day one starts at 10:00 in the studio with your manager and buddy.
2. Set up your laptop with IT; enable disk encryption and the password manager.
3. Read this handbook end to end, then ask your buddy about anything unclear.
4. Shadow two client calls and one design review.
5. Join the weekly team meeting on Tuesday.

## Week two

- Pick up a small, real task on a live project with your buddy's help.
- Book 30-minute intro calls with each team lead.
- Have a check-in with your manager on Friday: what is going well, what is not.

## Where you work

New and existing staff may work remotely at most 2 days a week. The other days
are studio days, so new joiners get to know the team in person. Agree your
remote days with your manager in your first week.

## Who to ask

- Laptop, accounts and access: IT (#it-help)
- Contracts, pay and leave: People Ops (#people)
- Everything else: your buddy
