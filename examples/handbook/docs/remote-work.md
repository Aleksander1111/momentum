# Remote work

We are a hybrid studio: we value time together in the studio and the focus
that working from home gives.

## How much

Everyone may work remotely up to 3 days a week. Tuesday is a studio day for
everyone, because the weekly team meeting and design reviews happen then.
Pick your remote days with your team so that each team has an overlap of at
least one studio day besides Tuesday.

## Working from abroad

You may work from another country for up to 20 working days a year. Tell
People Ops at least two weeks ahead so they can check tax and insurance rules.

## Equipment

- The studio provides a laptop, monitor, keyboard and headset.
- You can claim up to 300 EUR once for a desk chair or desk.
- Internet costs are covered by the monthly home-office allowance of 25 EUR.

## Availability

Core hours are 10:00 to 16:00 in your local time zone. Keep your Slack status
up to date and answer direct messages within two hours during core hours.

## Security

Use the VPN on public networks, lock your screen when you step away, and never
leave your laptop in a car. Report a lost device to IT the same day.
