# Meetings

## The weekly team meeting

The whole studio meets every Tuesday at 10:00 for 45 minutes, in the studio
kitchen and on the video call for anyone who cannot be there.

1. Wins of the week (10 minutes): each team shares one thing it shipped.
2. Project updates (15 minutes): only blockers and changes of plan.
3. Studio news (10 minutes): People Ops and the founders.
4. Open questions (10 minutes): anything posted in #weekly beforehand.

The facilitator rotates every month between the team leads. Notes go in the
shared "Weekly meeting" doc the same day, so anyone who missed it can catch up.

## Meeting habits

- Every meeting has an agenda in the invite; no agenda, no meeting.
- Default to 25 or 50 minutes so people get a break between calls.
- Wednesday afternoons are meeting-free for focus work.
- Decline a meeting if you cannot add or take anything from it.
- Whoever booked the meeting writes down decisions and owners at the end.

## Client meetings

Client meetings are booked by the project lead and need at least two people
from the studio. Share notes with the client within one working day.
