# Weekly meeting

The whole studio meets every Tuesday at 10:00 for 45 minutes, in the kitchen and on video: wins, project blockers, studio news, then questions posted in #weekly. The facilitator rotates monthly between team leads; notes are shared the same day.

Meeting habits:

- No agenda, no meeting; default to 25 or 50 minutes
- Wednesday afternoons are meeting-free
- The organiser records decisions and owners
- Client meetings need two studio people; notes go to the client within a day
