# Onboarding

A new joiner's first two weeks.

- **Before day one:** laptop and welcome pack sent home, accounts invited, a buddy from another team assigned
- **Week one:** day one at 10:00 in the studio; laptop setup with IT; read the handbook; shadow client calls and a design review; join the Tuesday team meeting
- **Week two:** a small real task, intro calls with each team lead, a Friday check-in with the manager
- **Where you work:** new and existing staff may work remotely at most 2 days a week; remote days agreed with the manager in week one
