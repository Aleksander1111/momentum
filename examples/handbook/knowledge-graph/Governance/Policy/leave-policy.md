# Leave policy

| Leave | Allowance |
| --- | --- |
| Holiday | 26 days a year plus public holidays, pro rata for part-time; up to 5 days carry over until 31 March |
| Studio closure | Christmas to New Year, not counted as holiday |
| Sick leave | Fully paid up to 30 days a year; a doctor's note after three days |
| Parental | 16 weeks for birth parents, 8 weeks for partners and adoptive parents |
| Other | 2 days for moving house, up to 5 days compassionate |

Holidays over three days are requested two weeks ahead.
