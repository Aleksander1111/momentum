# Weekly meetings

Every manager holds a weekly one-to-one of 30 minutes with each person who reports to them.

- The report owns the agenda: workload, growth, feedback in both directions
- Skipped at most once a month; a cancelled one-to-one is rescheduled within the same week
- Notes are private to the two of them
