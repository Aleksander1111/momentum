# Remote work policy

- **How much:** everyone may work remotely up to 3 days a week; Tuesday is a studio day for all, and each team keeps one more shared studio day
- **Abroad:** up to 20 working days a year, announced to People Ops two weeks ahead
- **Equipment:** laptop, monitor, keyboard and headset provided; a one-off 300 EUR for a desk or chair; 25 EUR a month home-office allowance
- **Availability:** core hours 10:00 to 16:00 local time; direct messages answered within two hours
- **Security:** VPN on public networks, lost devices reported to IT the same day
