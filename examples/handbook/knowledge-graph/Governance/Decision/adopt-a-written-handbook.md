# Adopt a written handbook

Decided on 2022-03-01 by the founders and People Ops.

- **Context:** policies lived in emails, slide decks and people's heads; new joiners got different answers from different people
- **Decision:** one written handbook in the repository is the source of truth; a rule that is not written there does not apply
- **Outcome:** old policies move to `archive/`, and changes go through pull requests
