# Survey on remote work preferences

An anonymous survey of all 30 staff on how many days a week they want to work remotely. **Delivered** on 2025-02-01.

| Preferred remote days | Share of staff |
| --- | --- |
| 0 to 1 | 20% |
| 2 | 37% |
| 3 | 33% |
| 4 or more | 10% |

Most want a fixed shared studio day; Tuesday was the most popular.
