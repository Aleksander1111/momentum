# Remote working policy (2019)

> Superseded by docs/remote-work.md in 2022. Kept for reference only.

## Eligibility

Staff who have passed their six-month probation may ask to work from home.
Requests are approved case by case by the managing director.

## How much

Approved staff may work from home one day a week, on Fridays only. Working
from home on other days needs approval each time.

## Equipment

Staff working from home use their studio laptop. No allowance is paid for home
equipment or internet costs.

## Availability

Staff working from home must be reachable by phone from 09:00 to 17:30 and must
attend any client meeting in person if asked.

## Review

This policy is reviewed every two years by the managing director.
