# Brightwater team handbook

How we work at Brightwater Studio, a 30-person product design studio. This
handbook is the written source of truth for our policies and habits; if it is
not written here, it is not a rule.

## Contents

- [Onboarding](docs/onboarding.md): your first two weeks
- [Remote work](docs/remote-work.md): where and when you can work from home
- [Leave policy](docs/leave-policy.md): holidays, sick leave and parental leave
- [Meetings](docs/meetings.md): the weekly team meeting and meeting habits

Old policies that no longer apply live in `archive/` for reference only.

## Changing the handbook

Anyone can propose a change by opening a pull request. People Ops reviews
policy changes; the team leads review everything else. Merged changes are
announced in #general on the following Monday.
